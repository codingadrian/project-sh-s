Trú — a proposed grammar

Trú is this project's name for a proposed, modernized form of Bruncájc: not a record of how the language is spoken today (that's Shá's), but a deliberate revival strategy — an attempt to ask what a living, growing Bruncájc could look like if it needed new grammar and new words for daily life in 2026, the way every living language quietly does.

No reformed grammar exists yet. What exists so far is groundwork: a phased research plan (Bruncájc Revival Project, foundation-plan track) that calls for building solid comparative and proto-Chibchan evidence before proposing any specific reforms, plus a neologism methodology and a proposal for a community language council that would hold real authority over what gets adopted. Only the first phase of that plan — comparative research — has been completed. No grammar rules have been proposed or published yet.

This project is an experiment in using AI to support language revival work, not a substitute for the language or for the Boruca/Bruncájc community's own authority over its future. Any reforms that eventually appear here are proposals for discussion, not a standard imposed from outside. See the About page for more on that framing.

What's already written

The working documents behind this track live in output/boruca/ and haven't been turned into published pages yet:

  • Revival foundation plan — a phased plan prioritizing comparative/historical evidence-gathering before any reform work begins.
  • Neologism framework — a decision procedure for coining new words (semantic extension, compounding, existing derivational suffixes, reconstruction from proto-Chibchan, cross-Chibchan borrowing), each proposal tagged by confidence.
  • Language council proposal — a proposed community body, modeled loosely on language academies elsewhere, to review and approve any standardization or new vocabulary before it's treated as more than a draft.

Until that process produces actual proposed grammar, this page will stay a placeholder. In the meantime, the real, documented grammar is at Shá's — Grammar.
